# Scope of Use

This project is for **educational and personal use only**.

It exists to help me learn network scanning, Docker, and basic security tooling by running it against **networks and devices I own or personally control** (e.g. my home lab/network).

## Authorized use
- Scanning devices and networks you own.
- Scanning networks/devices you have explicit, documented permission to test.

## Not authorized
- Scanning any network, device, or host you do not own or have explicit permission to test.
- Any use against third-party infrastructure, public networks, or systems belonging to others.

Unauthorized scanning of networks you do not own or control may be illegal. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the right to scan any target before using these tools.
